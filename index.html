Book One

When you know the Earth is dying, what will you do to save it?

Tessa Horton and Tú Chen discover they possess supernatural abilities and are admitted to the College of Geological Evolution — a secretive international school managed by an ancient organization: the Keepers of the Rock.

The Keepers, bestowed with powers connected to the Earth, have a duty to protect the planet and its creatures, so humanity can reverse its accelerating course toward destruction. But they are forbidden from directly meddling in human affairs — and must rely on covert guidance to save mankind.

Standing against them: the Debilis — monsters who prey on humanity and manipulate it toward their ultimate goal — control of all Earth’s resources, and humanity itself.

If you love secret worlds, crystals, minerals, history, or science, you’ll love this immersive 4‑D reading experience.

About the author

E.K. Wise

Mother, traveller, and now author — E.K. Wise’s endless curiosity about cultural history and geology developed in her youth after living abroad and traveling internationally.

She is enticed by multicultural cuisine and will sample almost any food once. When not creating stories, she’s likely wrangling teenagers, sweating on her stationary bike, reading, or studying cool rocks and minerals.

Wise passionately advocates for learning evaluation, as she and three of her four kiddos (and maybe the dog) have ADHD. She earned degrees in psychology and clinical work and lives with her family in Southern California.
